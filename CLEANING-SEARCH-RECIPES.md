# Cleaning tender search recipes

Prepared 26 November 2026 by Contract Alerts, the operator of the linked matching service. This is a first-party practical resource, not an independent review or a list of live tenders.

Use a keyword search and a CPV classification search separately, where the official portal supports them. A number entered into a keyword box is not necessarily a category filter.

| Work | CPV code, without check digit | Suggested keywords |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General cleaning | 90910000 | cleaning services |
| Building cleaning | 90911200 | building cleaning |
| Windows | 90911300 | window cleaning |
| Offices | 90919200 | office cleaning |
| Schools | 90919300 | school cleaning |

Codes: [official CPV classification](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2002/2195/pdfs/eur_20022195_2009-08-07_en.pdf#page=259). Keywords are our suggested starting points. Use the portal's category picker if it presents a hierarchy. A code is not evidence that the notice is suitable for your business.

1. Start with the service you actually deliver.
2. Narrow by the delivery locations your staff can reach.
3. Separate notices accepting a response from awards and future-pipeline information.
4. Read the scope, lots and deadlines in several results before saving a search or choosing notifications.

[Find a Tender](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/) and the [Cabinet Office saved-search guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-short-guides/buyers-and-suppliers-how-to-use-the-central-digital-platform-the-enhanced-find-a-tender-service-html#searching-for-opportunities-and-setting-up-notifications) provide an official UK starting point. Search relevant regional portals as well.

## Brief to adapt

> We provide routine office and public-building cleaning in [delivery area]. Our team can cover [number of sites or travel limit]. We also provide [specialist work we actually deliver]. We want to review cleaning lots that fit those services.

This fictional brief needs your real capabilities in place of the brackets. It is not a customer example or a promise of available work.
