# IT-support tender search and first-review worksheet

Prepared 26 November 2026 by Contract Alerts, the operator of the linked tender-matching service. This free first-party worksheet is a suggested review routine, not a buyer's specification or a list of live tenders. Copy it into your own notes; no account is needed.

## Find a notice to review

Search the [official sources for your delivery country](https://getcontractalerts.com/countries). UK starting points include [Find a Tender](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/) and the relevant regional portal; [TED](https://ted.europa.eu/) covers European notices. National portals can carry work absent from TED.

| Search starting point | CPV code without check digit | Suggested keywords |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Helpdesk and support | 72253000 | IT helpdesk; service desk; systems support |
| Computer-related services | 72500000 | managed IT services; ICT management |
| Computer support and consultancy | 72600000 | IT support; technical computer support |

Source: [official CPV classification](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2002/2195/pdfs/eur_20022195_2009-08-07_en.pdf). The keyword combinations are our suggestions. Try separate searches using the portal's category controls. Entering a number in a keyword box may not apply a CPV filter. Broad codes can include unsuitable work; narrow codes can miss a relevant lot.

Our [stored IT-support notice examples](https://getcontractalerts.com/tenders/it-support#current-notices) use CPV prefix `725`. Searches under the other codes can find work outside that example list. Check the official notice for changes, withdrawal and the current deadline.
