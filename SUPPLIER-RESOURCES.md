# Using notice counts to find work your business can deliver

Published by Contract Alerts, 22 November 2026. These are first-party supplier resources, separate from
this repository's static 21 November 2026 aggregate snapshot. Contract Alerts sells a tender-matching
subscription; the official search instructions and checklist linked here are free to use without signing up.

## Start with an actual notice, not a total

A count in the saved CPV table is a broad classification total from our imports. Division `90` includes
more than cleaning, and division `77` includes more than the grounds-maintenance work a particular firm
can deliver. A notice can have several classifications, and source records can overlap. Neither count
establishes a unique procurement, an available lot, a suitable contract or a business's eligibility.

Use a specific service search and open the official notice. Check the place of delivery, lot structure,
scope, buyer documents and response deadline. Contract values can cover multiple years or lots, or be a
framework ceiling rather than guaranteed spending. A future deadline in a stored record does not prove
that a procurement has not since been changed or withdrawn.

## Current sector starting points

- [Cleaning tender notices and alerts](https://getcontractalerts.com/tenders/cleaning):
  public records classified under CPV `9091`, with buyers, stated values, future response deadlines and official links.
- [Grounds-maintenance tender notices and alerts](https://getcontractalerts.com/tenders/grounds-maintenance):
  public records classified under CPV `773`, plus practical searches and questions about sites, travel and seasonal capacity.
- [IT-support tender notices and alerts](https://getcontractalerts.com/tenders/it-support):
  computer-related services recorded under CPV `725`, with questions about support hours, systems, handover and security requirements. This narrow list excludes work recorded only under other IT classifications.
- [Refurbishment tender notices and alerts](https://getcontractalerts.com/tenders/refurbishment):
  overhaul/refurbishment records under CPV `45453`, with questions about sites, trades, access, programme and pricing. It is not a list of all construction work.

These site lists are generated from stored notice records when requested; they are not saved snapshot
downloads or personalised matches. The supported direct imports are Find a Tender and Contracts Finder.
They exclude notices without a usable future deadline, can contain cross-source overlap and do not
cover every procurement portal. Check the official notice and any relevant regional portal yourself.
